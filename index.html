Maram Sakr

Incoming Assistant Professor
Department of Computing Science
University of Alberta · Starting January 2027

My research lies at the intersection of robot learning, machine learning, and human-robot interaction. I am particularly interested in data quality and curation for robot learning, learning from human demonstrations and feedback, multimodal human-robot interaction, social navigation, and foundation models for robotics.

I received my Ph.D. through a joint program between the University of British Columbia and Monash University, advised by Elizabeth Croft, Dana Kulić, and Mike Van der Loos. Previously, I was a visiting graduate scholar at Stanford University and Johns Hopkins University.

Recruiting thesis-based MSc and PhD students for Fall 2027!
Interested in joining my research group? Please read about my research and complete the prospective student interest form. Formal admission through the UAlberta Computing Science graduate program is also required.
